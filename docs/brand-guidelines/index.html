06Reference interpretationPROPOSED

Two references, two jobs.

Left: how it feels. Right: what it must do. Never copy either.

A · DESIGN REFERENCE — FEEL
Why it feels modern:
type does the talking.
  • Expressive lowercase headlines with tight leading + one colour word
  • Huge whitespace; sections breathe, rhythm changes every scroll
  • Cutout people breaking container edges → movement
  • Small graphics (dots, ticks, arcs) used once, with restraint
  • Buttons chunky-friendly, arrows that nudge on hover
STEAL THE SPIRIT · NOT THE PURPLE/YELLOW, LOGO, OR LAYOUTS
B · BUSINESS REFERENCE — FUNCTION
What it must do:
guide to a hello.
  • Condition-first architecture (weight · sugar · gut · hormonal)
  • Consultation journey explained in 3 calm steps
  • Expert face + credentials near every claim
  • FAQ that dissolves fear before the form
  • Short lead form, always one tap away
LEARN THE ARCHITECTURE · NEVER THE ORANGE/TEAL, CARDS, OR NAV
